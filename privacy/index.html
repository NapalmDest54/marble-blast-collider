Privacy Policy

Marble Blast Collider · Effective October 6, 2026

Marble Blast Collider (the "Game") is made by Greenfield Labs LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the Game uses, why, and the choices you have. In short: we don't ask for your name or email, we don't run our own servers, and your saved game stays on your device.

Information the Game keeps on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, lives, boosters, Shooters, settings and purchase records) is saved in a file on your device so you can continue where you left off. It is not sent to us. Deleting the Game deletes this data. Because it lives only on your device, we can't recover it if the device is lost or the Game is deleted. Purchases of permanent items (such as packs and Shooters) can be restored with the "Restore Purchases" button.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see or store your payment details. The store tells the Game whether a purchase succeeded, and the Game records which items you own on your device.

Advertising

The Game is free and shows ads. Ads are delivered by Unity LevelPlay and the ad networks it works with (for example Unity Ads, Google AdMob and AppLovin). To show and measure ads, these providers may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android), IP address, device model and settings, and how you interact with ads. Their use of this information is governed by their own privacy policies, including Unity's privacy policy.

On iOS, the Game asks for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before ads can use your device's advertising identifier. If you decline, you'll still see ads, but they won't be personalized using that identifier. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. On Android, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings. Buying "No Ads" turns off banner and between-level ads for 30 days (the Premium Pack includes the same 30 days). Ads you choose to watch for rewards remain available.

Analytics

To understand how the Game is played and to fix problems, the Game sends gameplay events to Unity Analytics (Unity Gaming Services), for example which level you started or finished, coins earned and spent, which offers and ads were shown, and purchases made. Along with these events Unity receives a randomly generated install identifier, your device's vendor identifier, device model, operating system and app version, language, and your country (derived from your IP address). The Game doesn't send your name, email address or advertising identifier with these events, and it has no account or sign-in. Unity processes this data on our behalf under Unity's privacy policy.

You can turn analytics off at any time with the "Share analytics" switch in the Game's Settings. To ask for the analytics data linked to your install to be deleted, contact us at the address below.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the Game schedules reminders on your device (for example, when your lives are full). These are created on your device and don't involve any data sent to us. You can turn them off in your device's settings.

Beta testing

If you test a pre-release version through Apple TestFlight, Apple shares limited information with us, such as your name or email address (as entered in your invitation), device and app version, crash reports and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send. We use it only to improve the Game. See TestFlight & Privacy.

Children

The Game is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through an ad provider, contact us and we'll help.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its use. Since we don't hold personal information about you, most requests concern the ad providers above, who you can contact directly. We're happy to help: contact us at the address below.
